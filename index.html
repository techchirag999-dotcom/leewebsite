About Lee Hydraulics
& Fasteners

Engineering Legacy

Over 40 years
of precision engineering.

Lee Hydraulics & Fasteners (P) Ltd. is a strategic spin-off of Lee Fasteners Pvt. Ltd., pioneers in industrial and aerospace forging since the 1980s.

The company brings high-grade forgings, robotic handling, CNC automation, and advanced precision engineering into critical defence, aerospace, mining, oil and gas applications.

Lee Fasteners Legacy
Industrial & aerospace forging since the 1980s
Our Journey

Forging capability
into the future.

1985
Early Titanium Innovation

Pioneered precision engineering solutions with early titanium product development for India's emerging space programs, laying the foundation for Lee Fasteners.

2000
India's First Titanium Fastener Manufacturer for ISRO

Became the first Indian company to manufacture and supply titanium fasteners for ISRO, establishing a trusted presence in the aerospace and defense sector.

2006
Aerospace & Defence Supply Record

Entered the defence manufacturing ecosystem through DRDL, delivering critical fastener solutions for missile systems and strengthening India's indigenous defence programs

2017
Major Capacity Expansion

Expanded manufacturing operations to a 100,000 sq. ft. facility, enabling higher production volumes and advanced engineering capabilities.

2023
Patented Technology Innovation

Achieved a breakthrough in mining-sector technology through proprietary innovation, securing patents and advancing industrial engineering solutions.

2025
Artillery Cell Manufacturing

Entered strategic defense manufacturing with specialized artillery cell production, supported by advanced quality systems and precision manufacturing processes.

Today
Engineering Excellence at Scale

100+ trusted partners across 8+ industries, delivering high-performance fastening and engineered solutions for aerospace, defense, mining, industrial, and strategic sectors.
